# Video Studio soundtrack manual walkthrough

1. Register a local source-media folder containing a supported MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, OGA, or Opus file, then open its video session in Video Studio.
2. In **Soundtrack controls**, choose **Choose audio**, scan the registered folder, select one trusted local file, and create the pending soundtrack proposal.
3. Verify the player and violet **Soundtrack lane** preview the working proposal while the displayed accepted revision remains unchanged.
4. In **Iterations**, confirm the enabled soundtrack operation. Verify a new accepted revision appears and the soundtrack remains in the dedicated lane.
5. Change timeline start, source trim, volume, or mute. Submit the adjustment and verify it again remains pending until confirmation.
6. Use **Replace** to select a different trusted file, confirm the replacement proposal, then use **Remove** and confirm removal.
7. During accepted-cut playback, seek before, into, and after the soundtrack range. Verify playback starts at the configured trim, respects placement, volume, and mute, and does not play pending audio after returning to the kept revision.

Intentionally absent: waveform, fades, looping, ducking, and beat sync.
